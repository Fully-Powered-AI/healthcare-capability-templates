# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Two published methods in CALLE-AI/awesome-phone-call-agents (MIT) supplied ideas. Its day-board confirmation method (skills/confirmcall-day-board): going through a whole day of existing bookings with a single contact for each, sorting every answer into coming, not coming or wanting to move, never counting an unanswered call or a voicemail as confirmed, making no repeat attempt the desk has not chosen, and leaving every diary change to a person. Its missed-appointment method (skills/carecall-missed-appointment): offering a patient who missed a visit only the times the practice has actually listed, giving no appointment details to anyone but the patient, accepting a no without pressing, not asking why the visit was missed, handing any health question to the practice’s own staff rather than answering it, keeping what is asked and shared to what booking a visit takes, with no medical detail, checking that a missed visit has not already been rebooked before following it up, and treating a request to be called back as no new call until the practice decides when. Fully Powered AI left out the automated dialling and built the work around the practice’s own exports and its own approved wording, and added the stop on export headers it cannot match, the stop on files that carry surnames, contact details, dates of birth or insurance numbers, withholding from the list any staff note that mentions a health matter, offering each open slot to one person only, the list of diary changes for staff and the fixed output format. No third-party text is reused.
