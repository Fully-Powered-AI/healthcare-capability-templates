# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. One published method shaped it: the hospital logistics method in kishorkukreja/awesome-supply-chain (MIT), in its hospital-logistics folder. From it come the idea of a par as the fixed amount each location is filled back up to at every restock, sized from average daily use over the days the stock has to last plus a buffer measured in days of use; handling lots so the one with the earliest date goes first, and putting a value on stock that will be lost; a warning period ahead of expiry; a refill trigger that fires before the regular restock; reading a recorded stock-out as a sign the par is set too low; and moving surplus or short-dated stock to where it is used faster. Fully Powered AI designed the rest: the limit to medical-surgical and office supplies, with medicines and everything else listed and left alone; room-by-room work from exports with set column headers that stop on a mismatch; use counted per day the clinic is open; a par that also covers the supplier's delivery time; the lot-by-lot count, in whole units, of how much of each lot each room will use before its date; the draft order net of open orders, rounded to supplier packs, with an approval line; and surplus in other rooms shown beside an order but never subtracted from it. The source's operating-room, implant, emergency-cart, medicine and compliance material was left out. No third-party text is reused.
