From the dental laboratory coordination method in CaseMark/skills (Apache-2.0) it takes four ideas: setting the date a lab expects to return a case against the date the patient is booked to receive it, having the work back early enough to be looked over before that visit, recording every remake together with why it was needed, and reviewing each lab once a quarter, including whether its cases came back by the promised date. Fully Powered AI wrote the front-office version and left out everything in the source about the lab order itself, shade, materials, inspecting the work and digital files: it reads one spreadsheet export with stated column headers and stops when one does not match, holds back a set number of days before each seating, sorts every case into fixed lists to act on, copies each remake reason exactly as the office wrote it and never classifies or judges it, builds each lab's quarter from the office's own dates with no targets or ranking, and contacts no one and books nothing. No third-party text is reused.
