From the medical staff credentialing method in CaseMark/skills (Apache-2.0) it takes the core contents of a clinician's file (licence, controlled-substance registration, board certification and a recurring exclusion-list check), confirming each item with the body that issued it rather than from the clinician's own copy, approvals that run for a fixed term with renewal work started a set number of days before they end, a completeness check of each file before anyone reviews it, and leaving privileges, peer review and judgements of competence to the medical leadership. Fully Powered AI wrote the version for a medical practice rather than a hospital, with every regulatory specific removed: it works from a roster and a spreadsheet export with stated column headers and stops when one does not match, takes every required item, lead time and accepted source from the practice and supplies none of its own, adds the malpractice insurance policy and payer enrolment as tracked items, calculates each due date and start date and shows both on every item, sorts items into fixed lists to act on, and contacts no one and submits nothing. No third-party text is reused.
